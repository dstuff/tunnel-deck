Visual inspection
Inspected all three icons at native 128, 64 and 32 px on light (#f5f6f8)
and dark (#1d222c) backgrounds using qa-preview.png.
The portal silhouette and cyan route remain distinct at 32 px.
The route-switch junction and highlighted branch remain distinguishable at 32 px.
The TD silhouette remains readable at 32 px; the route inside D becomes a small
cyan accent at that size, with the tunnel detail clearer at 64 and 128 px.
The tile and principal symbols remain distinguishable on both backgrounds.

Transparency inspection
All source images and delivered PNGs have genuine alpha transparency.
All four canvas corners are fully transparent.
No detached pixels remain at alpha >= 16/255 in any delivered PNG.
At alpha 1..15/255 the generator leaves faint detached pixels in the padding:
portal: 5893 pixels; route switch: 1747 pixels; monogram: 357 pixels.
These do not present visible specks in the inspected native-size comparisons.
Strictly empty exterior alpha is therefore not yet verified; the chosen design
should receive final edge preparation before application-icon packaging.
